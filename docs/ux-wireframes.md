# UI/UX Wireframes

## Brand Direction

Art Decor Events should feel elegant, reliable, and operationally clear.

Visual language:

- Black and warm gold from the logo
- Soft ivory, stone, and champagne neutrals
- Restrained use of venue/event photography
- Clean cards, compact data tables, professional icons
- Light and dark mode from the beginning

## Login

Use a split login composition:

- Left: warm, high-quality company image with subtle overlay.
- Right: focused login panel with logo, role tabs, and clear form fields.

The UI must never rely on image text for important information.
